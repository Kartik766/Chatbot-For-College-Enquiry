About RCPIT

R. C. Patel Institute of Technology, Shirpur was established in 2001 by the Shirpur Education Society. It is an autonomous institute affiliated to Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU), Lonere, approved by AICTE and accredited by NAAC with Grade A. Address: Near Nimzari Naka, Shahada Road, Shirpur, Dist. Dhule, Maharashtra.

Admission

Admission to B.Tech is based on MHT-CET or JEE scores through the State CET Cell, plus institute-level seats. Cutoffs vary by round, category and branch. Confirm fees and dates on the official notice. Use the chat assistant for documents required, fee structure and seat matrix.
